HOW DO I OPEN IT?
Download from Etsy → Purchases. Unzip, then double-click "CalmStudio Desk.html". On mobile, open the link in the Start Here PDF in Safari/Chrome → Add to Home Screen.

MY DATA DISAPPEARED.
Almost always a different browser, a moved file, or private mode. Re-open the file exactly as before, or Settings → Restore backup. Tip: export a backup weekly.

PHONE AND COMPUTER DON'T MATCH.
Data is private per device. On the source device: Settings → Export backup. On the other: Settings → Restore backup.

DOES IT CONNECT TO MY BANK?
No. You enter your own numbers. Nothing leaves your device.

CAN I CHANGE CURRENCY / COLOURS?
Yes. Settings has 10 currencies (locked once you add money records, so pick first). The top bar switches between 6 palettes and light/dark at any time.

HOW DO I SAVE AN INVOICE AS PDF?
Invoices → the ⎙ print button → choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

CAN MY TEAM USE IT?
The licence is for one user. Each person needs their own copy.

REFUND?
Digital files can't be returned, but message me. I'd much rather fix what isn't working.
